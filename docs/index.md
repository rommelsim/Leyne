# Departly

Live Singapore bus and MRT arrivals — see the next bus at a glance, watch it
move along its route, and get a nudge one stop before yours. Free, with no
sign-up and no account.

**Get Departly:**

- [App Store (iOS)](https://apps.apple.com/sg/app/id6770481761)
- [Google Play (Android)](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.leyne.leyne)

**What it does:**

- Live arrival times for every SG bus stop, straight from LTA DataMall
- Track your bus along its route — live position and minutes to *your* stop
- "Alert me 1 stop before" — a nudge so you never miss your alight
- MRT station crowd levels, live, plus today's crowd forecast
- Nearby stops and stations with walk distances
- Save the stops you use; reorder them your way
- Home Screen widgets on both platforms; Live Activity countdown on iOS
- Service alerts: train line status and station lift outages

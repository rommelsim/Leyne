# Departly — Support

**Departly** shows you live Singapore bus and MRT arrivals — save the stops
you use, watch your bus move along its route, and get a nudge one stop
before yours.

For help, questions, bug reports, or feedback, email
**leyne0000@gmail.com**. We aim to reply within a few business days.
Please include your OS version (iOS or Android) and device model, and a
screenshot if relevant.

## Frequently asked questions

**Wasn't this app called Leyne?**
Yes — Departly is the same app under a new name. Your saved stops and
settings carry over with normal app updates; nothing to redo.

**No buses are showing for a stop.**
Arrivals come live from LTA DataMall. Outside service hours, or if LTA has
no prediction for that service yet, a stop can legitimately show nothing.
Pull to refresh, check another stop, and confirm you have a network
connection.

**It can't find my location / no nearby stops.**
Departly needs location access to show nearby stops and stations:

- **iOS:** Settings ▸ Departly ▸ Location ▸ While Using the App
- **Android:** Settings ▸ Apps ▸ Departly ▸ Permissions ▸ Location ▸
  Allow only while using the app

You can still search stops by name, bus number, MRT station, or postal
code without location.

**How do I save a stop?**
Open the stop (from Nearby or Search) and tap the **bookmark** in the top
corner. Saved stops live in the **Saved** tab with live arrivals; press
and hold a card there to drag it into your preferred order. Saved stops
also still appear in Nearby when you're close to them.

**How do I get an alert before my stop?**
When you're tracking a bus on its route screen, tap **"Alert me 1 stop
before"** at the bottom. You'll get a notification as the bus approaches
the stop before yours. Notifications permission is asked once during
onboarding; you can change it later in system settings.

**What do the MRT crowd bars mean?**
Station screens show the platform crowd **live** (Low / Moderate / High,
from LTA's real-time feed) and a **forecast for the rest of today** in
half-hour steps, so you can time your trip around the crush. Late at
night, after the last train window, the forecast reads "Service has ended
for today" — that's expected, not a bug.

**What does the ARRIVING capsule mean?**
Any service whose next bus is about a minute or less away gets the
ARRIVING treatment on its row — that bus is pulling in now.

**Where are the widgets?**
On your Home Screen, on both platforms:

- **iOS:** Pinned Stop, Nearby, and Favourite Service widgets — plus a
  **Live Activity** countdown on the Lock Screen and Dynamic Island while
  you track a bus.
- **Android:** Stop and Nearby home-screen widgets — long-press your home
  screen ▸ Widgets ▸ Departly.

**Why do I see ads, and are they tracking me?**
Departly is free and shows ads via Google AdMob. On first launch you're
asked for consent — a Google consent form where it's required (e.g. the
EEA/UK), and on iOS, Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt. If you
**allow** tracking, ads can be personalized using your device's
advertising identifier; if you **decline**, you still see ads, but
non-personalized ones. You can change this anytime:

- **iOS:** Settings ▸ Privacy & Security ▸ Tracking
- **Android:** Settings ▸ Security & privacy ▸ More privacy & security ▸ Ads

See the privacy policy for full details.

## Privacy

Departly stores your saved stops and settings only on your device and has
no user accounts. Full details: see the Privacy Policy linked alongside
this page.
